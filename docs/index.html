Your Google connection, on your computer

Cloud-enabled builds let you connect Google Drive through Google's own sign-in screen. One remembered connection works in Download and Export. ClipHarbor never asks for your Google password.

By default, Drive access is limited to files you use with ClipHarbor. The app creates a dedicated export folder. Account connections use your operating system's credential protection.

Version 0.6.1 adds resizable Media, preview, Properties, and timeline areas, with the entire video always visible. Facebook sharing prepares your video and opens Facebook to finish posting to your own profile or managed Page. The personal-profile flow was verified with a real private test post. Drive sign-in, upload, restart, and token renewal were also verified.
