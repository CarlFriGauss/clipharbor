Download from the page you are watching

The optional Chrome and Edge companion offers video links, formats, computer/Google Drive destinations and download progress. ClipHarbor starts quietly when needed; downloads continue after the popup closes and appear in the app's Activity. The extension uses temporary access to the page you invoke, with no browser cookie or general browsing history access.

The Windows installer includes its local bridge and extension files. Load the extension once through your browser's Extensions page. This GitHub release is not listed in either browser store. Setup instructions · Extension ZIP

Your Google connection, on your computer

Cloud-enabled builds let you connect Google Drive through Google's own sign-in screen. One remembered connection works in Download and Export. ClipHarbor never asks for your Google password.

Choose a destination through Google's folder picker in Download, Export, or the downloader extension. The shared choice is remembered. Drive access stays limited to files you use with ClipHarbor, and account connections use your operating system's credential protection.

The editor offers resizable Media, preview, Properties, and timeline areas, with the entire video always visible. An optional, separate Facebook companion can attach the video, enter your caption, verify your chosen audience, and post through your signed-in browser. This experimental feature requires Chrome's powerful debugger permission; the ordinary downloader does not. Existing drafts and unrecognized controls stop posting, and uncertain posts are never automatically repeated. Manual Facebook preparation remains available. Optional Facebook companion ZIP.
