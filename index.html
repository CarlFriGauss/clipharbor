Download from the page you are watching

The optional Chrome and Edge companion offers video links, formats, computer/Google Drive destinations and download progress. ClipHarbor starts quietly when needed; downloads continue after the popup closes and appear in the app's Activity. The extension uses temporary access to the page you invoke, with no browser cookie or general browsing history access.

The Windows installer includes its local bridge and extension files. Load the extension once through your browser's Extensions page. This GitHub release is not listed in either browser store. Setup instructions · Extension ZIP

Choose audio and subtitles

Version 0.9.4 fixes the reported YouTube translated-subtitle download error. The app keeps the same YouTube session open for a 65-second preparation wait before fetching a translation. Activity shows a countdown and Cancel remains available. Original-language captions and other sites have no added delay; no new runtime dependency or video processing is needed for this wait.

Inspect a video in ClipHarbor or its extension to choose the audio language and downloadable subtitles. Creator captions, automatic captions and available translations are labelled; subtitles start at None. Facebook languages appear only when the source exposes them for download.

Version 0.9.4 defaults to Original source and selectable subtitles. Ordinary downloads copy both chosen video and audio streams without re-encoding, including when adding captions or choosing a dubbed language. If a requested container cannot hold those codecs, MKV is used with a clear notice. Source resolution, frame rate, channels and compressed media quality are preserved. Audio-language choices follow the source's quality ranking.

Burn into picture remains an explicit conversion: changing video pixels requires re-encoding and cannot preserve its original compressed video. Keeping 4K/60 fps only preserves dimensions and frame rate; this can take much longer than playback time. Activity displays the actual current stage percentage and provides Cancel. There are no new runtime dependencies, local AI models or GPU requirements. Source rate limits and authentication requirements still apply.

Your Google connection, on your computer

Cloud-enabled builds let you connect Google Drive through Google's own sign-in screen. One remembered connection works in Download and Export. ClipHarbor never asks for your Google password.

Choose a destination through Google's folder picker in Download, Export, or the downloader extension. The shared choice is remembered. Drive access stays limited to files you use with ClipHarbor, and account connections use your operating system's credential protection.

The editor offers resizable Media, preview, Properties, and timeline areas, with the entire video always visible. An optional, separate Facebook companion can attach the video, enter your caption, verify your chosen audience, and post through your signed-in browser. This experimental feature requires Chrome's powerful debugger permission; the ordinary downloader does not. Existing drafts and unrecognized controls stop posting, and uncertain posts are never automatically repeated. Manual Facebook preparation remains available. Optional Facebook companion ZIP.

The Drive folder chooser, export, direct download, destination verification and temporary download cleanup passed live account checks. Facebook automatic video attachment, caption entry, Only me selection and publication confirmation were also verified in Chrome. Version 0.9.4 prevents repeated export clicks or repeating an old retry action from starting duplicate jobs. Browser layout variants may still need adapter updates. Drive sign-in, restart, and token renewal were verified in earlier versions. Direct Page API uploads and X still require live validation; the YouTube upload test is deferred while the test account has a temporary restriction. Read the release notes.
